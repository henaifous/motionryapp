Privacy policy · Android

Your heart stays on your phone.

Motionry records your rides, runs and walks on your Android phone and Wear OS watch. There is no account and no Motionry server holding your activities. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and this page explains what stays on your device, what the app sends, and why.

Heart rate, routes and Health Connect records are never sent to us, to analytics or to advertisers.

Analytics Anonymous usage only.

Google Analytics for Firebase counts screens and features, never who you are.

Ads Google AdMob, your choice.

What stays on your phone

Everything Motionry records is stored on your Android phone. That includes:

  • Your rides, runs and walks: GPS route, heart rate, speed, pace, elevation, steps, laps and pauses
  • Activity names you type and your heart rate zones
  • Settings such as your sports, heart rate source and paired strap

Motionry has no account and no server of its own, so none of this is uploaded to us.

Wear OS. If you record with the Motionry watch app, the activity travels from your watch to your phone through the Wear OS data layer in Google Play services, between your own devices. The watch app has no internet access of its own.

Android backup. When backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android can include Motionry's activities and settings in your Google backup and carry them to a new phone. That backup lives in your Google account under Google's own privacy policy, and Motionry does not see it.

Permissions

Each permission is explained in the app before Android asks, and each one can be skipped. Motionry keeps working with whatever you allow.

Location
Precise and approximate, only while you use the app or record. Draws your route and measures speed and distance. While you record, a notification stays visible and tracking keeps working with the screen off. GPS stops when the activity ends.
Physical activity
Counts steps to show cadence and stride while you run or walk.
Nearby devices
Asked only when you connect a heart rate strap such as Polar H10. Used to find and connect to the strap, never to work out where you are. A Wear OS watch works without it.
Notifications
Shows the recording notification with your live stats, and alerts such as the Zone 5 alert.
Health Connect
Optional. Reads and writes the records listed in the next section.
Photos and files
None. Images and videos you save go to your gallery through Android's media store, which needs no access to your photos. Motionry cannot see your other photos or files.
Internet
Not asked. Needed to load maps, ads, analytics and app settings.
Advertising ID
Not asked. Google AdMob uses it to show and measure ads, as described under Ads.

Health Connect

Health Connect is Android's own store for health and fitness data. Motionry connects to it only if you choose to, and only for what you allow.

Reads
Heart rate, resting heart rate and weight, to set your zones and show recovery. You can allow each one separately.
Writes
Your rides, runs and walks as exercise sessions, with their route, heart rate and active calories.
Restores
Motionry reads back only the workouts it saved itself, to rebuild your library after a reinstall.

Data read from or written to Health Connect is used only to run Motionry's features on your device: zones, recovery, workouts and charts.

Health Connect data is never sent to analytics, never shared with third parties, never sold, and never used for advertising, including by Google AdMob.

You decide what Motionry can read and write, and you can change it at any time in Android Settings › Health Connect › App permissions › Motionry, or from the You tab in the app.

Analytics

Motionry uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and decide what to improve. The data is anonymous: it is not linked to your name, email or Google account.

What the app sends

  • Screens opened and features used, such as starting, pausing or finishing an activity, exporting, sharing, or connecting a sensor or Health Connect
  • Rounded activity totals: sport, duration in minutes, distance to the nearest 0.1 km, and whether heart rate and steps were recorded
  • App choices: your sports, the type of heart rate source (watch, Bluetooth strap or none), whether Health Connect is connected, which map is in use, settings you switch, whether you allowed each permission, and onboarding steps completed or skipped
  • How a video before sharing went: how many were asked for, and whether they played or were not available

What Firebase adds automatically

  • A random app instance ID, reset if you uninstall and reinstall the app
  • Device model, Android version, app version and language
  • Approximate country or region, derived from your IP address
  • First open and sessions
  • Your advertising ID, only where your ad choices below allow it

Motionry also asks Firebase Remote Config for app settings, such as which map to use. This request carries the same app instance ID.

What is never sent

  • Your name, email or anything you type, including activity names
  • GPS coordinates or routes
  • Heart rate values or any Health Connect record
  • Bluetooth device names, which can be personal

Google Analytics follows your ad choices. Where Google's consent form applies, its ad-related uses (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) stay off until you agree in the form, and you can change your answer under Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere they are on, so Analytics can use your advertising ID and Google can use usage data to measure and improve ads. Analytics data is not sold. Google keeps it for no longer than 14 months. You can read how Google handles it in the Firebase privacy and security page.

Ads

Motionry for Android is free and has no purchases or subscriptions. It is supported by ads from Google AdMob, in two places:

  • Banners on the details screen of an activity
  • Short videos before you share an image, a video or an award. The share button says so before you tap it, for example “Watch video & share”. If a video cannot load, you can share anyway.

What AdMob receives

To show and measure ads, Google collects and uses data from your phone: your advertising ID, your IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Motionry does not give AdMob any information about your activities, routes, heart rate, health data, name or email, and it does not pass on your location. Google may infer an approximate area from your IP address.

Your consent and choices

  • Consent form. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places where the law asks for it, Google's consent form appears when you first open the app. No ad is requested until you have answered.
  • Change your mind. Open the You tab › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. The row appears wherever the consent form applies.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset it or delete it in your phone's settings, usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads. Ads then stop being personalised.

Motionry is not directed at children, and its ads are not meant for them. To learn how Google uses data from apps like this one, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob privacy and security page.

Maps and other services

CARTO and OpenStreetMap
Draw the maps in the app and in share images, using the open-source MapLibre library. To load map tiles, CARTO's servers receive the area being shown along with standard device and connection information, such as your IP address. The map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors. See the CARTO privacy policy.
Esri
Provides the satellite view. When you choose it, Esri's servers receive the area being shown and your IP address. See the Esri privacy statement.
Google Firebase
Anonymous analytics and app settings, as described above.
Google AdMob
Ads and the consent form, as described above.
Google Play services
Location, Wear OS sync and Health Connect run through Android and Google Play services, under Google's privacy policy.

Sharing and export

Map images, videos, stickers and GPX files are created on your phone. They go only where you send them: your gallery, or any app you pick in Android's share sheet. Once shared, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies. Motionry cannot see what happens to them afterwards.

Your choices

  • Skip or revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Motionry › Permissions, or in Health Connect for health data.
  • Choose your ad settings from Ad privacy choices in the You tab, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete an activity from the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all its data from your phone and reset the analytics app instance ID. A copy in your Google backup stays until you delete that backup in your phone's backup settings. Workouts already saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect.
  • Ask us about the analytics data or request its deletion using the contact below.

Motionry is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

This website

The Motionry website uses no cookies and no analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. If you join the Android waitlist, your email is used only to send one message when Motionry is on Google Play.
